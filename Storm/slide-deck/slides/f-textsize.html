text items on Course sections render smaller than 12px

styles.css: 8px ×4 · 9px ×10 · 10px ×24 · 11px ×26

Cause: Font sizes are fixed pixels, mostly 8–12px.

Impact: Hard to read, especially for older professors. Raising the browser’s default font size does nothing: a table cell stays 11px even at double size. Only page zoom helps.

Suggestion: Switch to rem units, with about 16px body text and a 14px minimum. Then recheck that the dense tables still fit.

This matches the first-impressions note: professors are older than we are and will have worse eyesight. The automated contrast scan passes, but size isn’t part of it.
